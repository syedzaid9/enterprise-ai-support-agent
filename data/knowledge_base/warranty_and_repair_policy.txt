ENTERPRISE HARDWARE WARRANTY & REPAIR GUIDELINES
Document ID: POL-WAR-2026-V2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Technical Quality, Engineering & RMA Operations
================================================================================

1. STANDARD 1-YEAR LIMITED HARDWARE WARRANTY
All physical hardware products and electronics sold through our official channels include a standard one (1) year limited manufacturer warranty starting from the verified date of retail purchase.

2. SCOPE OF WARRANTY COVERAGE
- Covered Scenarios:
  a) Internal electrical failures, PCB defects, and soldering component degradation.
  b) Mechanical switch, zipper, button, or structural assembly faults arising from ordinary intended use.
  c) Battery capacity degradation exceeding 30% within the first 12 months under standard charge cycling.
  d) Factory cosmetic blemishes identified upon initial unboxing and reported within 7 days.

- Excluded Scenarios (Warranty Void):
  a) Water submersion, chemical corrosion, or fire exposure on non-ruggedized units.
  b) Accidental drops, shattered screens, cracked chassis, or structural trauma caused by negligence.
  c) Unauthorized third-party repairs, aftermarket modifications, firmware tampering, or broken warranty seals.
  d) Normal aesthetic wear and tear (scuffs, light scratches, fabric fading).

3. EXTENDED PROTECTION PLANS (ENTERPRISE CARE+)
- Customers who enrolled in Enterprise Care+ at the time of purchase receive two (2) to three (3) years of extended warranty coverage.
- Enterprise Care+ includes accidental drop and liquid spill protection with a standardized $25.00 service deductible per incident (maximum 2 claims per 12-month period).

4. WARRANTY SERVICE CLAIM PROCEDURE
Step 1: Contact support with Order ID, customer details, product serial number, and a description of the malfunction.
Step 2: A support specialist conducts remote diagnostic triage.
Step 3: If hardware failure is confirmed, an authorized RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number and prepaid shipping kit are generated.
Step 4: The customer sends the unit to our Regional Repair Depot.

5. REPAIR TURNAROUND TIMELINES & REPLACEMENTS
- Diagnostic & Repair Window: Typical turnaround is seven (7) to ten (10) business days from depot check-in.
- Unit Replacement: If the defect cannot be repaired within 14 business days, a brand-new or certified refurbished unit of equal or greater specifications is dispatched with expedited shipping.

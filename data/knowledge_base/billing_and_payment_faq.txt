1. SUPPORTED PAYMENT METHODS & CURRENCIES
We accept the following payment instruments for all retail and enterprise orders:
- Major Credit & Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB, and Diners Club.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express Checkout.
- Flexible Financing (BNPL): Klarna, Affirm, and Afterpay (available for eligible orders over $35.00).
- Enterprise Invoicing (Net 30): Available for verified commercial accounts with approved credit lines.

2. STATEMENT BILLING DESCRIPTOR
- All authorized charges and card settlements appear on credit card and bank statements under the merchant descriptor:
  "ENT*SUPPORT-RETAIL" or "ENTERPRISE-MERCH-DIRECT".

3. TAXES, INVOICES & VAT EXEMPTIONS
- Sales Tax Calculation: Sales tax and VAT are automatically computed at checkout in compliance with state, provincial, and national destination tax codes.
- Tax Invoices: Official PDF commercial invoices with breakdown of line items, shipping fees, and tax IDs are emailed automatically and can be re-issued by support agents on request.
- Tax Exemption: Non-profit and resale organizations can submit valid state tax exemption certificates to support for tax-free account setup.

4. TEMPORARY PENDING AUTHORIZATIONS & FAILED CHARGES
- When an order attempt fails or is cancelled immediately, a temporary pre-authorization hold may appear on the customer's online banking view.
- These pending holds are not actual charges; the financial institution will automatically release the funds within 24 to 72 business hours depending on the bank's internal processing rules.

5. CHARGEBACK PREVENTION & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
- We are dedicated to fair, rapid dispute resolution. Customers experiencing billing discrepancies, unrecognized charges, or duplicate transactions are encouraged to contact our support desk directly before initiating bank chargebacks.
- Our team can issue immediate refunds or credit adjustments, saving customers the 60-to-90-day bank investigation turnaround.

ENTERPRISE RETURN & REFUND POLICY

1. OVERVIEW & POLICY STATEMENT
Our commitment is to ensure complete customer satisfaction with every purchase. This document outlines the official terms, conditions, timeframes, and procedures for product returns, refund evaluations, and financial reimbursement.

2. ELIGIBILITY TIMEFRAME
- Customers may request a refund or return within thirty (30) calendar days from the confirmed delivery date of the shipment.
- For items purchased during special holiday promotional periods (November 15 to December 24), the return window is extended to January 31 of the following year.

3. QUALIFYING REASONS FOR REFUND ELIGIBILITY
Refunds and returns are fully authorized under the following circumstances:
a) Defective Products: The item has manufacturer hardware faults, technical defects, or fails to perform according to stated specifications.
b) Damaged During Delivery: The merchandise arrived broken, crushed, cracked, or compromised due to carrier transit mishandling.
c) Incorrect Product Received: The customer received an SKU, color, size, or model different from what was ordered.
d) Inaccurate Product Description: The delivered item materially differs from the specifications, features, or imagery presented on the official product listing.
e) Missing Components/Accessories: The package arrived with essential parts, manuals, or bundled items missing.

4. NON-ELIGIBLE ITEMS & EXCLUSIONS
Refunds will NOT be granted under the following conditions:
a) Customer-Inflicted Damage: Products showing signs of impact damage, liquid submersion (unless rated waterproof), intentional alteration, or improper electrical usage.
b) Expired Window: Return requests initiated after the thirty (30) calendar day delivery window.
c) Hygiene and Personal Care Items: Opened in-ear headphones, health devices, or intimate apparel unless delivered defective.
d) Digital Goods & Software: Downloadable software licenses, digital gift cards, or redeemable subscription codes once activated or viewed.
e) Customized/Personalized Merchandise: Custom-engraved, made-to-order, or personalized goods unless there is an error made by our manufacturing team.

5. RETURN MERCHANDISE AUTHORIZATION (RMA) PROCESS
To initiate a return:
Step 1: The customer contacts support and provides their Customer ID and Order/Transaction ID.
Step 2: For damaged or defective claims, the customer may be asked to provide clear photographic evidence or diagnostic descriptions.
Step 3: Support issues an official RMA number and a prepaid return shipping label (for defective/damaged/wrong item claims).
Step 4: The customer securely packages the item and drops it off with the designated carrier (FedEx, UPS, or USPS).

6. REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINES & METHODS
- Inspection Phase: Upon receipt at our fulfillment center, items undergo technical inspection within two (2) to three (3) business days.
- Reimbursement Method: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the customer's original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Buy-Now-Pay-Later account).
- Financial Settlement Timeframe: Once approved by a supervisor or processed in our billing system, funds typically post to the customer's account within five (5) to ten (10) business days, depending on their financial institution.
- Alternative Store Credit: Customers may elect to receive instant Store Credit with an added 10% bonus credit, valid for twelve (12) months.

7. RESTOCKING FEES & RETURN SHIPPING COSTS
- Defective, Damaged, or Company Error: 100% free return shipping and 0% restocking fee.
- Buyer's Remorse / Preference Returns (unopened/like-new): A flat $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the total refund amount. No restocking fee is applied if returned in original retail packaging.
